# Butter-Fried Oyster Mushroom Miso Soup with Leek (1 liter)

## Ingredients

* 150 g oyster mushrooms, torn into bite-size pieces
* 1–2 tbsp butter
* 1 liter water
* 1 vegetable stock cube
* 1 leek, sliced diagonally, 1 cm thick
* 1 scallion
* 1/2 tsp grated ginger
* 1/2 tsp grated garlic
* 2 tsp miso paste
* Sesame oil, to finish
* 2 small coriander leaves, to finish

## Method

1. **Fry the mushrooms:** Heat the butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the oyster mushrooms and fry until golden brown. Set aside.
2. **Make the broth:** In a pot, bring 1 liter of water to a boil and dissolve the vegetable stock cube.
3. **Cook the leek:** Add the diagonally sliced leek to the boiling stock and cook until it starts to soften (about 3–5 minutes).
4. **Add scallion and aromatics:** Slice the scallion. Add the **white part** to the pot along with the grated ginger and grated garlic. Let it heat through briefly (about 30–60 seconds).
5. **Turn off heat and add miso:** Turn the heat off. Put the miso paste in a cup, ladle in a little hot broth, and stir until smooth. Pour the mixture back into the pot and stir gently. (Don’t boil after adding miso.)
6. **Serve:** Ladle the soup into bowls. Add the fried mushrooms, sprinkle with the scallion greens, drizzle with a little sesame oil, and garnish with two small coriander leaves.

## Notes

* If your stock cube is salty, you can start with **1/2 cube** or use a bit more water.
* For extra punch, fry the mushrooms until they’re quite browned—this adds lots of depth.
