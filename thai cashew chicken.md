## Thai Cashew Chicken (Gai Pad Med Mamuang)

A sweet-salty, glossy stir-fry with crunch from the cashews—perfect over rice.

### Ingredients (serves 2–3)

**Stir-fry**

* 3 chicken thighs (boneless), sliced into bite-size pieces
* Cashews (untoasted), ~½–1 cup
* 1 onion, sliced
* 3 cloves garlic, chopped
* 1–2 chilies, sliced (use 1 for mild, 2 for spicy)
* 3 green onions, cut into ~5 cm strips
* Bell pepper, sliced (optional)

**Sauce**

* 2 tbsp oyster sauce
* 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
* 1 tbsp fish sauce
* 1–2 tsp sugar (to taste)
* 3 tbsp water

**To serve**

* Steamed rice

### Prep

1. **Mix the sauce** in a small bowl: oyster sauce + dark soy + fish sauce + sugar + water. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
2. **Slice everything** so it cooks fast: chicken, onion, garlic, chili, (bell pepper), green onion.

### Cook

1. **Toast the cashews (quick stovetop method):**
   Heat a dry pan (or a tiny splash of oil if you prefer). Add cashews and stir constantly until lightly golden and fragrant, 2–4 minutes. Remove to a bowl.
   *This makes them crunchy and prevents them from turning soft in the sauce.*
2. **Sear the chicken:**
   In the same pan/wok, add a little oil over medium-high heat. Add chicken in a single layer and cook until mostly done and lightly browned, 4–6 minutes.
3. **Aromatics:**
   Add onion, garlic, and chili. Stir-fry 1–2 minutes until fragrant and the onion starts to soften.
4. **Veg (optional):**
   Add bell pepper and stir-fry 1 minute.
5. **Sauce + gloss:**
   Pour in the sauce. Toss and simmer 30–60 seconds until it coats everything and looks shiny. If it gets too thick, splash in a bit more water.
6. **Finish:**
   Add green onions and the toasted cashews. Toss 20–30 seconds—just until the green onions soften slightly.

### Serve

Spoon over hot **steamed rice**. Add extra sliced chili on top if you want more heat.

**Tips**

* If your oyster sauce is very salty, use the lower end of fish sauce or sugar.
* Keep the cashews crunchy by adding them right at the end (as written).
